Content Strategist

We're hiring someone who doesn't know how to make boring content.

Neuroid is looking for a Content Strategist who lives and breathes content — the kind of person who watches a reel and instantly clocks the hook, the cut, and why it's working.

The Gist

You'll own content strategy across multiple brands, pitch creative concepts, and bridge the gap between performance creatives and brand storytelling. We expect you to already be tracking trends, formats, and platform shifts — for fun.

About Neuroid

Neuroid is a growth studio for D2C operators catalyzing transformative partnerships. A full-funnel growth partner helping brands scale with performance marketing, ad commercials/brand campaigns, UGCs/performance creatives, CRO & retention.

We've worked with brands like Lifelong, Woodenstreet, Kisah, Superbottoms, Yoho, Jewelsmars, Silverfied, NourishYou, DeepImpact, Truth&Hair and more.

What you'll do

  • Build content strategies for brands across industries — formats, hooks, narratives, distribution.
  • Conceptualize creatives for both performance marketing (CTR-driving, conversion-focused) and brand campaigns (narrative-led, top-of-funnel).
  • Pitch ideas and decks directly to clients — this is a client-facing role.
  • Work closely with creators, editors, and designers to bring ideas to life.
  • Stay on top of trends, formats, and platform shifts.
  • Translate brand briefs into content that actually performs.

What we're looking for

  • 2–4 years at a content/marketing/creative agency, having worked across multiple brands.
  • Sharp understanding of creator economy, content formats, and platform-native storytelling.
  • Strong communication — written, verbal, and "explaining a wild idea to a skeptical client".
  • Solid grip on what makes performance creative different from brand creative.
  • Comfort with ambiguity and fast turnarounds.

Bonus points

  • That creative-ADHD brain where ideas don't stop showing up uninvited.
  • Have personally created or scripted content (not just briefed it).
  • Know your way around tools like Notion, Frame.io, or basic editing software.
